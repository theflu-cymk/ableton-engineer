How this cabinet works

Each shot costs 1 token, and a hit is not a catch. Every fish carries its own per-hit catch chance, solved so that any fish you land on returns the same expected value: small fish are caught often, the kraken almost never. A shot that hits nothing pays nothing, so your aim moves the realised return. Move to aim, click or tap to fire.
